Short answers
  • US firm, mid-size or larger buildings Use Revit. Your structural and MEP consultants will expect RVT.
  • BIM on a Mac Use Archicad. Use Vectorworks Architect if you draw a lot of 2D or landscape, or want the lower price.
  • Houses, design-build and remodels Use Chief Architect Premier. Use Archicad or Revit if you also do commercial work.
  • Concept design and small projects Use SketchUp. Add Rhino for freeform or computational design.
  • Drafting and consultant backgrounds Use AutoCAD LT. Full AutoCAD is only worth it for 3D or its toolsets.
  • Renderings Use Enscape inside the model. Twinmotion is free for firms under $1M revenue. Use V-Ray for final marketing images.
  • On Linux Use BricsCAD BIM. It is the only tool here with a native Linux version.
What changed this year

Nearly everything is now a subscription. Rhino is the only major tool still sold as a one-time purchase, and BricsCAD still offers both. Graphisoft and Vectorworks are retiring their remaining perpetual licenses.

Autodesk now bundles Forma's early-stage design tools with Revit, and every major vendor has added an AI assistant or AI rendering. A new generation of browser-based tools is aiming at Revit directly. They are compared on Emerging BIM.

Once you know your software, the Computer Chooser picks the machine to run it on. Prices are rounded and change often. Check the vendor before you buy.
