Forma

Autodesk · Site and early design
  • Web
Status
Building Design released April 2026
With Revit
Native connection, Revit becomes a Forma client

Included with Revit and the AEC Collection

Autodesk's cloud platform for site analysis and schematic design, and the company's stated successor to Revit "over time". If you pay for Revit, you already have it.

Early-stage only for now, with no construction documents. Expect the same lock-in as Revit, and charges for moving large amounts of data out.

Snaptrude

Snaptrude · Browser BIM, concept to schematic
  • Web
Status
Released, AI features since October 2025
With Revit
Two-way RVT exchange, by its own account
Price
Free for 3 projects. $60 a month individual, $100 a user a month for firms

Models from a brief or a sketch in the browser, with live drawings and several people in a model at once. The most direct fit for feasibility and schematic design in a Revit office.

Detailed documentation still usually moves to Revit. Test its Revit round trip on one of your own projects before relying on it.

Speckle

Speckle · Data layer
  • Web
Status
Released, open source

Connectors for Revit, Rhino, Grasshopper and more

Free tier and self-hosting. Team $99 a month

Moves model data between Revit, Rhino and the newer tools, with version history, a web viewer and dashboards. It is how most firms will run new tools beside Revit.

Not a design tool. It needs someone in the office who is comfortable setting up data workflows.

Opens large RVT and IFC models fast in the browser for coordination, quantities and editing, and now produces annotated plans, sections and elevations. AEC Magazine calls it the most mature of these for production work.

No construction details yet. Pricing by building area, not by seat, suits teams with many occasional users.

The best-funded Revit challenger, built by former Autodesk and Revit leaders. Several people model in the browser at once, AI agents can model and check requirements, and sheets are live views of the model.

Its library of building objects is still thin, and nothing goes back to Revit. Pilot it on a small project before committing a team.

Short answers
  • Revit office, earlier design Use Forma first, since you already pay for it. Pilot Snaptrude for schematic design with a team.
  • Piloting a Revit replacement Try Motif on a small project. Use Qonic if your work is coordination of big RVT and IFC models.
  • Feasibility with clients Use Arcol.
  • Interiors and space planning Use Rayon for drawing, Hypar for test fits.
  • Using several of these beside Revit Connect them with Speckle.
What is happening

AEC Magazine groups these tools as BIM 2.0: browser-based, several people in one model at once, and built on data rather than files, so AI can work with the model. They exist because Revit is a file-based desktop app that is hard to share live, and because firms are unhappy with Autodesk's prices and control of their data.

None of them produces a full construction set the way Revit does today, and most now pitch themselves as working beside Revit rather than replacing it. Autodesk said in September 2026 that Forma will replace Revit over time, with no date. Graphisoft has announced its own cloud collaboration layer and an Archicad connection to Forma. The question AEC Magazine now raises is whether AI agents doing the modeling will matter more than any of these interfaces.

Prices are early-stage and change often. Check the vendor before you buy. For the established tools, see BIM & Design Platforms.

Watch list

Not yet ready to compare: NeoBIM, AI-first BIM from a co-founder of Enscape, shown at NXT BLD 2026. Skema, an AI design assistant that syncs Rhino and Revit. Swapp, AI that drafts design development and construction sets inside Revit. Graphisoft's browser design tool for massing and layout, announced in 2026.
