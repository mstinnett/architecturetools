BIM software, from 1982 to now

When each design tool arrived, what it does today, and what to watch next.

Updated October 2026

Timeline

Desktop drafting in the 1980s, model-based BIM from the late 1980s and 2000, renderers in the 2010s, and browser-based tools since 2020. A year is a first release unless the entry says otherwise.

First release Rename, acquisition or standard c. Approximate year
  1. 1980s
    1. 1982 AutoCAD Autodesk ships it in December, for CP/M and the IBM PC
    2. 1984 Radar CH Graphisoft, for the Apple Lisa. Renamed ArchiCAD in 1987
    3. 1984 Allplan Nemetschek, 3D building models for architects and engineers
    4. 1985 MiniCAD Graphsoft, on the Mac. Renamed VectorWorks later
    5. 1986 MicroStation Bentley Systems, version 1, after PseudoStation in 1985
    6. 1987 ArchiCAD Radar CH released as ArchiCAD, the "Virtual Building"
  2. 1990s
    1. 1992 Chief Architect Home design on Windows 3.1
    2. 1996 IFC First version of the open model exchange format, from the alliance Autodesk started in 1994
    3. 1998 Rhino McNeel, version 1.0 in October
    4. c. 1998 VectorWorks MiniCAD renamed at version 8. Sources give 1998 and 1999
  3. 2000s
    1. 2000 Revit Revit Technology Corporation, version 1 in April
    2. 2000 SketchUp @Last Software, in August
    3. 2002 V-Ray Chaos, first version, for 3ds Max
    4. 2002 Revit to Autodesk Bought for $133 million
    5. 2004 Digital Project Gehry Technologies, building design on CATIA
    6. 2004 BricsCAD Bricsys, version 1, a DWG editor
    7. c. 2005 Twinmotion KA-RA, founded 2004. A second version was out by about 2008
    8. 2005 buildingSMART The IFC alliance takes its current name
    9. 2006 SketchUp to Google Bought from @Last Software
    10. 2007 Grasshopper Released in September as Explicit History, for Rhino
  4. 2010s
    1. 2010 Lumion Act-3D, in December
    2. c. 2011 Dynamo Visual programming for Revit, begun by Ian Keough. Shown in 2009, staffed by Autodesk in 2011
    3. 2012 SketchUp to Trimble Bought from Google
    4. 2015 Enscape Real-time rendering inside Revit, first as a beta
    5. c. 2015 Speckle Begins as open-source research at UCL's Bartlett
    6. 2015 BricsCAD BIM Bricsys adds BIM to its DWG engine
    7. 2016 Spacemaker Founded in Oslo, AI site planning
    8. 2018 Hypar Founded by Ian Keough and Anthony Hauck
    9. 2018 Higharc Founded, design software for homebuilders
    10. 2019 Twinmotion to Epic Games Bought from Abvent and made free for most firms
  5. 2020s
    1. 2020 D5 Render Out of public beta and on sale at version 1.6, in May
    2. 2020 Spacemaker to Autodesk Bought for $240 million
    3. 2021 Rayon Founded in Paris, shared 2D plans in the browser
    4. 2022 Snaptrude Version 1.0 in December, founded 2017
    5. 2022 Enscape joins Chaos The makers of Enscape and V-Ray merge
    6. 2023 Qonic Open beta, from the founders of Bricsys
    7. 2023 Forma Spacemaker released as Autodesk Forma
    8. 2025 Motif First product, browser model review, in March
    9. 2025 Arcol Public launch in June, founded 2021
    10. c. 2025 Pascal Editor Open-source browser building editor, in beta
    11. 2026 Forma Building Design Schematic design in Forma, included with Revit
    12. 2026 Forma to replace Revit Autodesk says "over time", with no date

The tools today

In the order they arrived. “Revit files” means it opens or exchanges RVT. “Shared model” means several people work in one model. Prices are rounded and change often, so check the vendor before you buy.

Browser-based tools

AEC Magazine calls these BIM 2.0: they run in the browser, several people work in one model at once, and the model is data rather than a file. None produces a full construction set the way Revit does today, and most now work beside Revit rather than replacing it.

Space to watch

Announcements from the last two years, newest first, from AEC Magazine, the vendors and the trade press. Each links to its source.

  1. 29 Sep 2026

    Rayon raises a €10 million Series A. Its fourth version, due by the end of 2026, adds 3D, generative tools and AI agents to its 2D plans. AEC Magazine

  2. 15 Sep 2026

    Autodesk says Forma will replace Revit “over time”, with no date. Revit becomes a Forma Connected Client, and Autodesk says it will keep developing it in the meantime. AEC Magazine

  3. 8 Sep 2026

    Motif releases Motif Design, browser BIM in which AI agents work in the same model as the team. Revit and Rhino models stream in, and nothing exports back to Revit yet. ENR

  4. Jul 2026

    NeoBIM, the AI design company led by Enscape co-founder Moritz Luck, shows its buildOS tools at the AIA 2026 expo. Architosh

  5. 2 Jun 2026

    Graphisoft announces a browser-based collaboration layer across the Nemetschek tools, with early access in 2026, and a native Archicad connection to Autodesk Forma. AEC Magazine

  6. 13 May 2026

    At NXT BLD in London, Arcol, Motif, Snaptrude, Qonic, Hypar and Autodesk share one BIM 2.0 panel, and Graebert demonstrates AutoDrawings, generated 2D drawings for Revit and IFC models. AEC Magazine

  7. 9 Apr 2026

    Qonic adds drawing generation: annotated plans and sections from BIM or IFC models that update with the model, exported to DWG. AEC Magazine

  8. 7 Apr 2026

    Autodesk releases Forma Building Design for schematic design, included with Revit and the AEC Collection. Autodesk

  9. 7 Apr 2026

    Revit 2027 adds Autodesk Assistant, which takes plain-language requests, and previews Revit as a client of the Forma cloud. Autodesk

  10. 25 Jun 2025

    Snaptrude 3.0 splits early design into four modes that work alone or together. Dezeen

  11. 2 Jun 2025

    Arcol launches publicly after a closed beta, for shared massing and feasibility in the browser. Business Wire

  12. 30 Jan 2025

    Motif, founded by former Autodesk co-CEO Amar Hanspal, raises $46 million. Its first product, for reviewing models in the browser, follows in March. VCA Online

Also in development

Skema, an AI design assistant that syncs Rhino and Revit. Swapp, AI that drafts design development and construction sets inside Revit. Nemetschek Creator, Graphisoft's browser tool for massing and layout, in early access.
