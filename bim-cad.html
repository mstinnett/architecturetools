Free tools: what else is free

The Free group in the matrix lists tools with no license fee for professional work, except SketchUp Free, which is for non-commercial use. Two other routes cost nothing: an education license, if you are a student or teacher, and a free viewer, if you only need to open what others send.

Education licenses

Most commercial tools are free while you study or teach. Education licenses are for learning only, and several mark every file and print they produce. A watermarked file stays watermarked once you open it in a commercial copy, so start real projects in a commercial license.

Autodesk Education
Revit, AutoCAD and the AEC Collection
Free one-year, single-user access for eligible students and educators, renewed each year while you stay eligible. For learning, teaching and research only, not commercial work.
Archicad Education
Windows · Mac
Free for students and teachers at accredited schools for a year, extended free until you finish your studies. Files carry an Educational watermark that cannot be removed.
Vectorworks Educational
Windows · Mac
A free Design Suite license for students and educators in selected countries. Educational files are watermarked, the watermark carries into files that reference or import them, and prints show it on every page.
Chief Architect student license
Windows · Mac
Chief Architect Premier, free for students in degree programs. The license runs to a set date each term and you reapply with proof of enrollment. Layout pages print with a Not for Commercial Use watermark.
SketchUp for Education
Web · iPad · desktop, by plan
Free for schools with a Google Workspace for Education or Microsoft Education account. Higher-education student and educator plans cost $55 a year.
Rhino education
Windows · Mac
Not free: $195 for a student or faculty license, $975 for a 30-seat lab. The same as the commercial version, but not transferable. Anyone can run the 90-day evaluation once, after which saving and plug-ins stop working.
