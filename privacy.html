Privacy & analytics

What this site collects when you use it, why, and how to refuse it.

Last updated 21 September 2026

In short

This site has no accounts, no forms, and no comments. Nothing you type into a tool leaves your browser. It sets no cookies. It counts visits with Plausible Analytics, which stores no personal data, and it keeps nothing that could identify you.

The tools run in your browser

The Computer Chooser and the Precise Unit Converter are ordinary web pages. The dimensions you enter, the software you select, and the budget you set are held in your browser only. They are not sent to this site or to anyone else.

Analytics

Visits are measured with Plausible Analytics, a service operated by Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia). Plausible processes and stores this site’s data on servers in Germany, inside the European Union, and acts as a processor for this site under its data processing agreement.

For each page you view, Plausible receives:

  • the page address and the address of the page that linked to it, if any;
  • your browser and operating system family, and a device type read from your screen width;
  • a country, region, and city derived from your IP address;
  • named events: which tool categories you used on that page, such as “Conversion: Metric to Imperial” or “Software: Revit”. Never the values you typed or your full configuration. The full list of event names is published in the site’s analytics documentation.

To count you once per day rather than once per page, Plausible combines your IP address and browser identifier with a random value that changes every 24 hours, and keeps only the resulting hash. The IP address and browser identifier are not written to any log, database, or disk, and the daily value is discarded, so visits cannot be linked from one day to the next. Plausible sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

The site only ever sees totals: how many people used a tool, and which categories they used. It cannot see, and does not try to see, any one person.

Why, and on what basis

Analytics are used for one purpose: to learn which tools and which parts of them are used, so that effort goes to the ones that are. This site does not advertise, does not profile, and earns no commission from any link.

Under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, the moment of processing described above rests on the site operator’s legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in understanding how a free, public tool is used, weighed against the minimal and momentary nature of that processing. No personal data is retained by this site or by its analytics provider.

Cookies and storage on your device

No cookies are set by this site or by its analytics.

One value may be written to your browser’s local storage, named scheme, holding “light” or “dark”. It is written only when you press the light/dark key, so that the choice you made follows you across the site’s pages. Until you press it, nothing is written and the page follows your system setting. It is never sent anywhere. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Fonts, scripts, and data are served from this domain, not from a third-party network. As with any web host, GitHub may record the IP addresses of visitors in its server logs for security and to meet its legal obligations, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site does not have access to those logs.

Links to other sites

Recommendations link to manufacturers and retailers. Once you follow a link, that site’s privacy terms apply, not these. None of the links are affiliate links, and no click is reported to the site you land on.

Your rights and how to refuse

Because no identifier is kept, this site cannot find the records of any one visitor, and so cannot single yours out to show, correct, or delete them. There is nothing to delete.

To refuse analytics altogether, block requests to plausible.io with a content blocker or your browser’s tracking protection. The tools work exactly the same without it.

If you are in the EU or the UK you also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to complain to your national data protection authority.

California and other US states

This site does not sell or share personal information, does not use it for targeted advertising, and does not retain any. It has no data to disclose, correct, or delete on request. Because nothing is sold or shared, there is no “Do Not Sell or Share” choice to make. Requests may still be sent to the contact below.
