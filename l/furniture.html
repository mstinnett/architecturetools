Furniture Fit

Parametric furniture with the clearances built in. Add a bed set, a dining table, bench desks, a gondola run — pick a size or type your own, drag it, and it snaps to furniture, to clearance zones, and to walls. Push a table against a wall and the wall-side chairs step aside. The zones are drawn, never assumed.

Solid outline = furniture · hatched = its clearance zone (included geometry — it snaps and it warns) · drag to move, double-tap to rotate · pinch or scroll to zoom · grid squares are 12″.
