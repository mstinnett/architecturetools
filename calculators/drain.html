Length · height · slope — enter any two

Height is the whole vertical space: the drop, the pipe's outside diameter and the allowance above it. The third of length, height and slope is calculated. Type in it and the oldest of the other two is calculated instead.

Every length on this page is held as a whole number of engine units. One unit is 1/960 of a millimeter, so a millimeter is 960 units and an inch is 24,384. Whole millimeters and sixteenths, thirty-seconds and sixty-fourths of an inch are all whole numbers of units, so metric and imperial inputs are both exact, and no step rounds in decimals along the way.
