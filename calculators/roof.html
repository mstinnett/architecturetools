Roof

How high is the roof at this point? Set the pitch, then drag along the section or type a distance or a height on the drawing, from the wall line, the drip edge or the ridge.

Drag along the roof, or type in a label on the drawing. Arrow keys move the point by an inch, with Shift by a foot.

Roof area

Every plane at one pitch has the same factor, so a hip roof and a gable over the same plan have the same roof area. No waste is included.

Hips and valleys

A low-slope roof? Roof Taper starts flat and slopes it to the drains. Only need a rise, a run or a slope? Slope solves any two of the three.

Every length on this page is held as a whole number of engine units. One unit is 1/960 of a millimeter, so a millimeter is 960 units and an inch is 24,384. Whole millimeters and sixteenths, thirty-seconds and sixty-fourths of an inch are all whole numbers of units, so metric and imperial inputs are both exact, and no step rounds in decimals along the way. A height is the plan distance times the slope, which usually falls between units: it is rounded to the nearest unit, then to the fraction you build to, and each row shows what that rounding adds or drops. Dragging moves the point in whole inches, or 10 mm in metric, and holds it at the wall line, the drip edge, the ridge and whole feet or meters as it passes them.
