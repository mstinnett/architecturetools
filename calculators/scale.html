Scale Resolution

A dimension taken through a scale carries a range, not a number. Reading a drawing, sheet error multiplies by the scale into the field. Measuring to draw, tape error accumulates across a run, and anything finer than the sheet resolves disappears into it. This shows the range you actually know — the digits past it are false precision.
