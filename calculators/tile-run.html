Uncut Tile Run

A run of whole tiles fits a window of wall lengths, not one length: the joints flex between their minimum and maximum. Give it a tile and a joint range and it draws those windows, locates your wall, and shows the slack. Near the middle of a window, field variation goes into the joints; at an edge, one tight course forces a cut.

Tile — the dimension along the run
Joint — minimum to maximum
to

The minimum joint is 3× the tile's actual facial variation (ANSI A108.02 / A137.1) — rectified porcelain runs under 1/16″, calibrated tile wider. A minimum that tight leaves nothing to adjust.
