Tile

A run of whole tiles fits a window of wall lengths, not one length: the joints flex between their minimum and maximum. Size The Wall returns the exact dimensions where the tile runs uncut, with the slack shown. Lay Out The Wall takes the wall as drawn and shows where the cuts land, flush or centered — each cut a range, because the joints flex.
