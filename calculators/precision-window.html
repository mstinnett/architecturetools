Precision Window

A run of whole tiles spans a window, not a point: the joints flex between their minimum and maximum. Give it a tile and a joint range and it draws the wall lengths where an uncut run lands, with the slack shown. Near a window's middle the setter absorbs field variation in the joints; near an edge one tight course forces a cut.
