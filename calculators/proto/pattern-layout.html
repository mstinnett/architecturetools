Pattern Layout

A pattern laid over a surface, with an accent band cutting through it. Drag the field to slide the pattern under the wall — the cuts move, the counts follow. Whole tiles draw plain; anything the boundary or the band slices is hatched. Counts come with both honest bounds: every offcut reused, and none.

Tiles are drawn at their lattice module (tile + joint), so herringbone's module reads ≈ nominal. Corner-sampling classifies whole vs cut — good enough to prototype the counting; the real engine would clip exactly.
