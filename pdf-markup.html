PDF Markup & Review

Software for redlining drawing sets, measuring from PDFs, and running reviews with your team and consultants.

Updated October 2026

The short answer

Bluebeam Revu is the industry standard. If your firm and your consultants already use it, stay on it. If you are leaving it, or never started, pick by platform and by whether you need to measure from the drawings.

  • Windows, firm already on Bluebeam Stay on Bluebeam Revu. Your consultants expect its markups and Studio Sessions.
  • Replacing Bluebeam for a team Get Drawboard Projects. It is built for drawing review across a firm, and guests join without a paid seat.
  • Replacing Bluebeam on your own, on Windows Get PDF-XChange Editor. Calibrated measurement and full markup for a one-time price.
  • On a Mac Use PDF Expert for markup. Bluebeam has no Mac desktop app. If you measure from drawings, get PDF Studio Pro or Acrobat Pro instead.
  • On an iPad with a pencil Use PDF Expert or Drawboard PDF. Use PolyPDF to measure. Use Documents to carry sets to site.
  • Already paying for Adobe Use Acrobat Pro before buying anything else. It measures and comments, it just isn't organized around drawing sets.

The software
Built for drawing sets
Bluebeam Revu
Windows · Bluebeam Cloud on web and iOS
The standard in architecture and construction. Tool chests of saved markups, a markups list you can sort and export, calibrated measurement and takeoff, sheet comparison, and Studio Sessions for live review with consultants.
There is no Mac desktop app, and Revu for iPad was retired at the end of 2025. Mac and iPad users get Bluebeam Cloud in the browser or the iOS app, which does less than Revu on Windows.
Drawboard Projects
Windows · web · iPad
The closest replacement for Bluebeam as a team review tool. Drawings live in one project with versions, markups sync live, and issues are pinned to a spot on a sheet with an owner and a status. Clients and consultants join as guests without a paid license.
Drawboard PDF
Windows · Mac · iPad · Android · web
Drawboard's single-user app, and the best pen experience on a Windows tablet. The free tier covers ink, shapes, text and signatures. Measurement is in the paid tier.
PDF-XChange Editor
Windows
The inexpensive Windows replacement for working alone. Clouds, callouts, stamps, and calibrated length, perimeter and area measurement, for a one-time license. It has no shared review.
Ncored
Windows · Mac
A PDF editor made by a working architecture studio for large CAD and BIM exports, the 50–200 MB sets that make general PDF apps lag on zoom and pan. Redlines, notes, shapes and large-format printing, with files kept on your machine. It has no iPad app, and it does not list calibrated measurement. Try it if Bluebeam or Acrobat stalls on your sets.
PDF Studio Pro
Windows · Mac · Linux
The Mac option if you measure from drawings and don't want a subscription. Markup, calibrated measurement and document comparison for a one-time license.
PolyPDF
iPad · iPhone
A new iPad app for drawings, and free. Callouts, discipline colors, and measurement of lengths, areas and angles at a set scale, with line detection to snap to the drawing. Sync and team review are not out yet. Use it to measure on an iPad now that Revu for iPad is gone.
General PDF editors
Adobe Acrobat Pro
Windows · Mac · iPad · web
Capable, and often already paid for. Comments, drawing markups, calibrated measurement and shared review all work. It has no tool chest or sortable markups list, so a fifty-sheet review takes more clicks than in Bluebeam.
PDF Expert
Readdle · Mac · iPad · iPhone
The best markup app on a Mac and on an iPad with an Apple Pencil. Fast on large sets, with highlights, shapes, ink, stamps and notes that sync across your devices. It does not measure to scale.
Foxit PDF Editor
Windows · Mac · iPad · web
An Acrobat alternative with measurement and shared review. Consider it if your firm buys Foxit for office documents already.
Reading sets and quick notes
Documents
Readdle · iPad · iPhone
A file manager that opens PDFs with annotation built in. Use it to carry a set to site, pull files from cloud storage, and note what you see on the sheet.
Preview & Markup
Apple · built into Mac, iPad and iPhone
Enough to circle something on one sheet and send it back. Nothing to install.
Sketching over drawings
Morpholio Trace
iPad · iPhone · Mac
Digital trace paper. Use it to sketch design responses over a plan or photo at scale, not to track comments.
Review inside a project platform
If the project lives in Autodesk Docs or Procore, mark up there. The comments stay attached to the sheet version the contractor builds from.
Fieldwire
Web · iPad · iPhone · Android
For construction administration and site visits. Markups and punch items pin to the sheet, work offline, and carry forward when a new revision is uploaded.
Newforma Konekt
Web · Windows
For the record around the review. RFIs, submittals, transmittals and project email in one place. Use it alongside a markup app, not instead of one.
Revizto
Windows · Mac · iPad
For reviewing the model, not the PDF. Issues are tracked across the 3D model and 2D sheets together, with clash detection for coordination.
Before you switch

Clouds, callouts, text and ink are standard PDF annotations, so they open in any of these apps. What does not carry over is the Bluebeam layer around them. Test these on one of your own sets first:

  • Calibrate a sheet and measure a dimension you know.
  • Mark up a set in the new app and open it in Bluebeam, then the reverse. Check that clouds, callouts and colors survive.
  • Rebuild the markups you use most. Bluebeam tool chests and custom markups-list columns do not import.
  • Run a review with one consultant. If they stay on Bluebeam, decide who exports and who imports.
