Before you switch apps

Clouds, callouts, text and ink are standard PDF annotations, so they open in any of these apps. Saved tool sets, markup lists and review sessions do not carry over. Test these on one of your own sets first:

  • Calibrate a sheet and measure a dimension you know.
  • Mark up a set in the new app and open it in Bluebeam, then the reverse. Check that clouds, callouts and colors survive.
  • Rebuild the markups you use most. Bluebeam tool chests and custom markups-list columns do not import into other apps.
  • Run a review with one consultant. If they stay on a different app, decide who exports and who imports.

Scaled measurement means you can calibrate a sheet to its drawing scale and take lengths and areas off it. Prices and plans change. Check the vendor before you buy.
