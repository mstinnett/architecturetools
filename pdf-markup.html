Also: review inside a project platform

Project platforms such as Autodesk Docs and Procore have their own markup. Comments made there stay attached to the sheet version the contractor builds from.

Procore
Web · iPad · iPhone · Android
On many projects the contractor runs the job here, and you join it rather than buy it: Procore charges the contractor or owner by construction volume, with unlimited users. The Drawings tool keeps the current set and its revisions, links callouts to their sheets, and overlays two revisions with removals in red and additions in blue. Your markups stay private until you publish them, and a cloud on a sheet can start an RFI. Drawings open offline on site. Its Inspections tool runs checklists, for field inspections rather than drawing review.
Fieldwire

Construction administration and site visits. Markups and punch items pin to the sheet, work offline, and carry forward when a new revision is uploaded.
Newforma Konekt

The record around a review: RFIs, submittals, transmittals and project email in one place. It works alongside a markup app, not instead of one.
Revizto
Windows · Mac · iPad
Review of the model rather than the PDF. Issues are tracked across the 3D model and 2D sheets together, with clash detection for coordination.
Before you switch apps

Clouds, callouts, text and ink are standard PDF annotations, so they open in any of these apps. Saved tool sets, markup lists and review sessions do not carry over. Test these on one of your own sets first:

  • Calibrate a sheet and measure a dimension you know.
  • Mark up a set in the new app and open it in Bluebeam, then the reverse. Check that clouds, callouts and colors survive.
  • Rebuild the markups you use most. Bluebeam tool chests and custom markups-list columns do not import into other apps.
  • Run a review with one consultant. If they stay on a different app, decide who exports and who imports.

Scaled measurement means you can calibrate a sheet to its drawing scale and take lengths and areas off it. Prices and plans change. Check the vendor before you buy.
