What should I use to mark up and review drawings?

Updated October 2026

Callouts, clouds and scaled measurement are in most paid PDF apps, on every platform here. The apps differ most in two things: marking up live with your team and consultants, and overlaying two revisions of a sheet to show what changed.

The marks describe each main app. Web, iPad and phone versions often do less. Measuring to scale means calibrating a sheet to its drawing scale and taking lengths and areas off it. A markups list here means every markup in a table you can sort, give a status and export. Prices are rounded. Check the vendor before you buy.

Not in the table: Preview, Okular and Xournal++ mark up a PDF for free, with no callouts, clouds or scaled measurement. Morpholio Trace measures to scale on an iPad but draws in pixels, for sketching over a plan rather than redlining it. Ncored, made by an architecture studio to open 50–200 MB CAD and BIM exports, does not list scaled measurement.

Before you leave Bluebeam

Clouds, callouts and text are standard PDF markup, so other apps open them. Custom markups-list columns and Studio Sessions work only in Bluebeam. PolyPDF imports Bluebeam tool chests (.btx). Test these on one of your own sets first:

  • Calibrate a sheet and measure a dimension you know.
  • Mark up a set in the new app and open it in Bluebeam, then the reverse. Check that clouds, callouts and colors are unchanged.
  • Rebuild the markups you use most, or import your tool chest if the app reads .btx files.
  • Review one set with a consultant. If they keep Bluebeam, decide who exports markups and who imports them.
